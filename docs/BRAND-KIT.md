- "GWA" is internal only. Never in customer-facing documents.
- Do not stylize as GEORGIAN WATER & AIR except in eyebrow/kicker text above a
  headline, which is the established site pattern.

This exact block is the single source of truth. Use it verbatim in every footer,
signature, proposal, and directory listing.

```
Georgian Water & Air
10 - 11 King Street
Barrie, Ontario  L4N 6B5

Toll-Free:  1.866.840.2789
Tel:        705.812.0320
Fax:        705.812.8228
Email:      info@georgianwaterandair.ca
Web:        georgianwaterandair.ca
HST:        764489076RT0001
```

**Number hierarchy.** The toll-free line is the *lead* number on print and
letterhead — it appears directly under the logo on the Sales Agreement. Use
`1.866.840.2789` as the primary customer-facing number. The 705 line is the
direct/local number and belongs in the detail block, not the headline position.

**Formatting.**
- Print, letterhead, forms, and footers: dot separators — `1.866.840.2789`
- Web body copy and `tel:` links: hyphens — `1-866-840-2789`, `705-812-0320`
- Do not mix conventions within a single document.

**Address formatting.** Two forms are currently in circulation:
`10 - 11 King Street` (Sales Agreement letterhead, BBB) and `11 King St #10`
(website footer). **Pick one and standardize.** Recommend `10 - 11 King Street`
since it already appears on legal documents. Until that decision is made, match
whichever form the surrounding document family already uses, and do not
introduce a third variant.

> **Standing flag — old address.** GWA is no longer at 761 Bayview Dr. Third-party
> directories (BBB, Yelp, LinkedIn, ZoomInfo, Canada247, Google) still carry it.
> No new asset may use it. Cleanup tracked in the NAP remediation work.

2. **GWA** — large, bold, dominant

### 3.2 Home Depot Home Services lockup

On program documents, the Home Depot orange box mark pairs with **HOME SERVICES**
and the line *Let us do it for you.* This is Home Depot's own lockup — reproduce
it as supplied, never rebuild or restyle it.

Placement convention: GWA at top-left, Home Depot Home Services centred, entity
and contact details right-aligned.

### 3.3 Association & partner marks

Appearing in the Sales Agreement footer: **FLAVOUR**, **Dockside Publishing**,
**OurHomes**, **CFIB**. Use only where the affiliation is current and relevant.
Confirm each is still active before carrying it onto a new template.

**Rules**
- Clear space: minimum one cap-height of the wordmark on all sides.
- Never recolour, outline, add drop shadow, or place on a busy photo without a
  scrim.
- Never stretch or alter proportions.
- The Home Depot badge is a partnership mark. Never lock it up as a single unit
  with the GWA logo, never resize it larger than the GWA logo, and never imply
  Home Depot authorship of a GWA document.

**Supporting brand graphics on file:** maple leaf icon, heart-shaped water
splash, residential/cottage/office service icons, water-background texture.

## 6. Voice & tone

**The voice is:** plain-spoken, warm, competent, local. Explains rather than
sells. Confident without hype.

**Do**
- Lead with the customer benefit, then the mechanism.
- Use concrete specifics: "7-stage", "since 2014", "over a decade".
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Reference the free water test as the low-friction next step.
- Say "your family", "your home", "your cottage" — second person, domestic.

**Don't**
- Fear-sell. No alarming language about water contamination.
- Use pressure or urgency framing ("act now", "limited time", "don't wait").
- Over-claim on health outcomes. Say what the system does, not what it cures.
- Use jargon without a plain-English gloss. "Reverse osmosis" is fine if the
  next clause explains it.

> **Why this matters:** existing negative reviews centre on perceived sales
> pressure. Brand language should actively counter that impression, not
> reinforce it. Every asset should read like an explanation, not a pitch.

## 9. Deliverable standards

### 9.1 PDF reports & analyses

- **Cover:** reverse logo on dark band, report title, period covered, prepared-by
  line, generation date.
- **Running header:** report title (left), page number (right).
- **Running footer:** `Georgian Water & Air · Confidential` plus source-data
  reference.
- **Every table:** column headers in the dark neutral with reversed text; zebra
  striping in the light neutral.
- **Every chart:** titled, axes labelled with units, source note beneath.
- **Executive summary first.** Findings before methodology, always.
- **Numbers:** Canadian conventions — `$1,234.56`, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD` in
  tables and `August 22, 2026` in prose.

### 9.2 Presentation decks

- 16:9. Title slide uses the reverse logo on a dark field.
- Content slides: primary logo bottom-right at small scale, or omitted entirely
  on full-bleed image slides.
- One idea per slide. Eyebrow → headline → supporting content, per §5.
- Body text no smaller than 18pt.
- Closing slide carries the canonical contact block from §2.

### 9.3 Customer-facing documents & proposals

- Letterhead: primary logo top-left, canonical contact block top-right or footer.
- Open with the customer's situation, then the recommendation, then the pricing.
- All pricing in CAD, tax treatment stated explicitly.
- Financing available — reference it as an option, never as pressure.
- Close with a clear, low-commitment next step.

### 9.3b Contractual & legal documents

Sales agreements and anything a customer signs carry requirements the marketing
material does not:

- Full print lockup per §3.1, including toll-free number.
- Entity name, full address, HST number, tel, fax, and email in the header block.
- Form number in a boxed mark, top-right (existing convention: `Form 1`).
- Page number, bottom-right.
- Footer notice, retained verbatim:
  `NOTICE TO BUYER: SEE YOUR RIGHTS UNDER THE CONSUMER PROTECTION ACT, 2002, ON BACK OF THIS AGREEMENT`
- HST line must show both the number and the rate: `HST #764489076RT0001 (13%)`

Never restyle, reword, or reformat a legal notice for visual consistency. If a
notice conflicts with the brand layout, the notice wins.

### 9.4 Social & marketing content

- Instagram/Facebook handle: `@georgianwater`
- Reverse logo on photo posts; primary logo on light graphic posts.
- Educational-first. Explain a water or air concept, then connect it to a
  solution.
- Hashtag base: `#GeorgianWaterAndAir #BarrieON #CleanWater #WaterTreatment`
- Always include a soft CTA to the free water test.

## 10. Data & reporting standards

These apply to every report, dashboard, and analysis produced under the GWA name.

1. **Traceability.** Every figure traces to a named source file, tab, and date
   range. State the source on the artifact itself, not just in conversation.
2. **Flag inferred values.** Anything derived, estimated, or assumed is labelled
   as such inline. Never silently interpolate.
3. **State the gaps.** If source data is incomplete, say so in the summary rather
   than producing a clean-looking number that hides a hole.
4. **Reconcile before reporting.** Totals cross-check against the source system
   before the artifact is built.
5. **Period labelling.** Always state the exact date range and whether figures
   are YTD, MTD, or period-only.
6. **No silent revisions.** If a number changes between versions, note what
   changed and why.

- Use urgency or pressure language in customer-facing copy
- Ship a report with an unlabelled inferred value
