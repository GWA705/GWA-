# Confirmation Script — UEI Financial Customers

Source: `Confirmation_Sheet_For_UEI.doc` (provided by the client). This is the
script the confirmer reads during a confirmation call. The digital confirmation
form mirrors it.

## Header
- Customer name
- # of calls
- City
- District
- Phone number

## Intro (spoken)
> Hi, may I speak with **[customer]**. My name is **[confirmer]** and I'm calling
> from TDC. Do you have a few minutes to go over your recent purchase of the
> **[product]** System?
>
> I should let you know that this call may be recorded for security purposes,
> okay. Congratulations on your purchase. Your application was approved. I just
> have a couple of questions for you:

## Questions (check each)
1. Is the **[product]** installed in your home and working fine?
2. Is the **[product]** performing the way the salesperson represented it?
3. Did you receive everything that you were promised?

## Terms (spoken + recorded)
> Now I need to quickly go over your terms: You are financing **$[amount]** over
> **[months]** months with your first installment of **$[installment]** coming
> out of the bank on **[date]**.

## Verify (check each)
4. These are the terms you agreed to?
5. This is your signature on the application?
6. You are aware that this is not a trial offer?

## Closing (spoken)
> If you have any questions or concerns, you can reach your sales office directly
> at the phone number on your delivery receipt or bill of sale. Or you can call
> our toll-free customer service line in Woodbridge, ON at **1-866-874-2532**.
> Is there anything I can answer for you today? Great — that's all I needed,
> thanks very much for your time.

## Footer
- Special arrangements
- Date / Time (recorded automatically)
- Confirmed by (the confirmer)
